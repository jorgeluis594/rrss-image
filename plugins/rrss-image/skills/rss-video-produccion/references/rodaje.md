# Shoot planning and footage review

Natural-looking short videos are planned: the shoot captures every scene from more
than one angle so editing can cut every 2–4 seconds without repeating a shot.

## Shot list order

Group shots by location, then by setup (camera position and light), then by script
order. Shooting all frontal-medium shots of one location together, then all detail
shots, avoids moving the light and camera repeatedly. Put shots with tight conditions
first (natural light that will change, talent with limited time, food that degrades).

Each shot has an ID `E<NN>-<letra>` from the scene number: `A` main shot, `B…`
coverage from the scene's `cobertura`. Add two shared IDs per location for extra
material: `X-<locacion>-broll` (5–10 s of product, hands, place details) and
`X-<locacion>-ambiente` (10 s of room tone, no one speaking).

## Minimum kit

- A recent phone with good rear camera, or a camera that records vertical 4K.
- Tripod with vertical mount; a gimbal only if the script has tracking shots.
- Wireless lavalier microphone per speaking person.
- One LED light with diffuser, or a window as key light; a reflector or white card.
- Spare battery and storage.

## Camera settings

- Vertical 9:16, 4K (2160×3840) at 30 fps; 60 fps only for shots marked
  `camara-lenta`. 4K leaves room for punch-ins in editing.
- Lock focus, exposure, and white balance per setup; clean the lens.
- Use the grid: eyes around the upper third; keep the subject and any action out of
  the bottom ~13% and right ~14% where platform controls cover the frame.
- Start recording 2 s before the action and stop 2 s after it ends.

## Light

- Light on the face from the front or slightly to the side; avoid a window behind
  the subject. Keep the same light within a setup so cuts between takes match.
- Product detail shots: soft side light that shows texture; avoid reflections on
  labels and screens.

## Audio capture

- Lavalier on every speaking person, checked with headphones before the first take.
- Quiet place; turn off fans and music; pause for traffic or announcements.
- Capture the real sounds the script asks for (frying, pouring, opening the box)
  close to the source.
- Record room tone per location (`X-<locacion>-ambiente`).

## Takes and direction

- Two or three takes per spoken line. For `literal: no` lines, encourage the actor to
  say it in their own words in at least one take; for `literal: sí`, at least one take
  must be word for word.
- Direct with the scene's `intencion`, not with line readings.
- Look into the lens for lines to camera, as in a video call with a friend.

## Continuity and product

- Note wardrobe, hair, props position, and product state at the end of each scene
  so later setups match (half-eaten food, opened box, poured drink).
- Clean product, labels facing the camera when they identify it, real contents only.

## People and places

- Real people on camera need consent for commercial use; minors need their guardian's
  consent. Note in `rodaje.md` who must sign before the shoot.
- Private locations need the owner's permission; avoid other brands' logos and
  identifiable bystanders unless they consent.

## Schedule estimate

Estimate per setup (lighting, framing, takes) rather than per scene, and add a buffer
for the first setup of the day. Tell the user the estimate is approximate.

## Reviewing footage

For each take, check and record in `material.md`:

| Check | Fail condition |
| --- | --- |
| Orientation and resolution | Not vertical, or below 1080×1920 |
| Focus and exposure | Face or product soft, blown highlights, crushed shadows |
| Audio | Voice unintelligible, clipping, noise, music in background |
| Action and line | Does not match the scene; a literal line changed |
| Framing | Key action or face in the platform-control areas |
| Product | Wrong variant, dirty, label unreadable when it matters |
| Continuity | Mismatch with neighboring scenes that editing cannot hide |

A take fails only on conditions that editing cannot fix. Mark the best take per shot
and list missing coverage or reshoots under «Pendiente».
