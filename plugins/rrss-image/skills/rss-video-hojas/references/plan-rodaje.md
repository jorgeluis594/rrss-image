# Plan de rodaje

For everyone at the shoot: when and where, who comes, what to bring, and the
order of the day. It is the plain version of `rodaje.md`; the technical table
stays in that file for editing.

## Sources

- `rodaje.md`: summary, step-by-step guides, blocks in shooting order, equipment,
  «Citación». Required.
- `VIDEO.md`: locations, talent, props, and the line of each shot.
- `storyboard/`: frames for each shot, and any location reference photo.

## Blocks, in order

1. `portada`: antetítulo with guion version and the shoot date; title of the
   video; `bajada` with what is recorded that day and what is prepared by others
   (generated or existing material), so nobody prepares it twice; `datos` with
   date, place, number of people, and estimated time.
2. `fichas` «Quién viene»: one card per person from «Citación»: arrival, place,
   clothes, what they bring.
3. `lista` «Qué llevar»: equipment, props, product, and clothes, merged and
   without repeats.
4. `figuras` «Cómo se arma»: the setup drawings or the location photo, when the
   plan describes the camera position.
5. `pasos` «Antes de empezar»: the preparation steps with their level.
6. One `escenas` block per recording block of the plan (same place and camera
   position), in shooting order, not script order: per shot, what is said or
   done, how many times, and the storyboard frame. The `tiempo` field can hold the
   block's estimated duration.
7. `aviso` «Al terminar»: where the files go and who to tell.

Leave out: shot IDs (use the scene number and a few words), camera settings the
person recording does not touch, prompts, and the technical table.
