# Review catalog for product sales images

Reviewed 2026-10-13. Apply these review criteria to the contract and selected composition.
The main SKILL.md defines the workflow and exceptions. A centered composition, bright
palette, illustration, or decorative typeface can be appropriate to the brand and job.
Aesthetic departures need a purpose, not permission by default.

All remedies below are changes to the complete prompt followed by full-piece
regeneration from original references. They never authorize editing, compositing,
adding a copy layer, or patching an already generated image.

## 1. Blocking defects: resolve before finished delivery

| Defect | Review and remedy |
| --- | --- |
| Base image completed with later text, offer, logo, or graphic overlays | Reject the staged workflow. Regenerate the complete advertisement in one operation with all required elements. |
| Changed product identity: print, trim, color, cut, label, decoration, parts, or packaging | Compare defining features with the real references. Correct or regenerate with those references; obtain a clearer source if needed. Small rendering and lighting differences are acceptable when the product remains the same. |
| Product looks pasted onto a separate background | Check light, perspective, contact, scale, and edges. Regenerate the complete advertisement with product references; never repair it by pasting the source photo or cutout. |
| Extra/missing limbs, melted accessories, floating objects, inconsistent shadows/reflections | Inspect visible anatomy and physical relationships. Repair the scene or regenerate without hiding the defect. |
| Incorrect copy, price, size, date, or claim | Compare with the brief and proofread the final export; regenerate the entire advertisement with the exact corrected copy. |
| Generated subject represented as a real customer, staff member, or testimonial | Correct the representation and use genuine evidence for factual claims. |
| A known asset restriction conflicts with the chosen depiction | Resolve the specific restriction or use an appropriate asset without falsifying product details. |

No style justification overrides product truth. Inspection is visual unless an actual
measurement or source comparison was performed and recorded. Fidelity means preserving
product identity and commercial features, not pixel equality.

## 2. Communication failures: judge the intended placement

| Signal | Question and possible remedy |
| --- | --- |
| Many callouts, badges, arrows, stickers, or competing text blocks | Can the viewer identify the message and product quickly? Reduce competing priorities or move detail to another slide. |
| Product partly hidden by text, logo, props, or crop | Are defining features and relevant details visible? Move the layout or use a suitable real detail photo. |
| Outlined, shadowed, decorative, or small type | Is it readable at delivery size and consistent with brand identity? Simplify treatment, enlarge, or provide a quiet background. |
| Weak contrast or content in interface overlays | Measure contrast where possible; verify placement-specific safe zones and preview the export. |
| Detail insets or promotional badges | Do they communicate necessary information? Keep them when useful and legible; remove redundant ones. |

Check the selected type’s blocks and reading hierarchy in its `references/tipo-<ID>-*.md` file.
A separate logo does not count as an advertising message. Required arguments,
conditions, and comparison labels must remain legible at the final viewing size.

## 3. Generic defaults: require a connection to the brief

| Pattern | What to examine |
| --- | --- |
| Repeated warm blurred room, centered pose, or symmetric framing | Does the setting or framing help show this product? A centered packshot may be exactly right. |
| Purple/orange palette, gradients, glow, chrome, clay, or seasonal clutter | Is the treatment rooted in brand assets, product, audience, or message? Simplify or redirect unexplained choices. |
| Plastic fur/fabric, universal rim light, artificial polish | Does texture match the real reference and does light have a plausible source? Do not add grain merely to disguise a defect. |
| Mascot, bubbly lettering, decorative symbols | Are these approved brand assets or helpful communication, or interchangeable filler? Preserve genuine identity. |
| Seamless neutral paper, hard flash, off-center product, film grain on every job | These alternatives can themselves become defaults. Choose them for the brief, not because they look less AI-generated. |
| Repeated subject, prop, breed, or layout | Does repetition build campaign recognition, or erase relevant product differences? Vary meaningful axes when needed. |

Name concrete evidence for specificity: a relevant feature made visible, a credible use
context, a real brand asset, or a layout serving the supplied offer. “Could a competitor
post it?” is a discussion prompt, not an automatic rejection test for catalog imagery.
Do not count aesthetic signals into a numeric failure score. Explain their combined
effect on this image and fix the actual problem.

## Prompt vocabulary

Words such as `masterpiece`, `premium`, `cinematic`, `8k`, `ultra-detailed`, `adorable`,
`professional`, and `eye-catching` often leave the intended decision unspecified.
Replace them with concrete light, materials, composition, or tone when those details
matter. They are not forbidden words; removing them alone does not improve the image.
Likewise, camera names, film stocks, hex values, and intentional imperfections are
steering cues, not guarantees of fidelity, authenticity, or measured quality.
