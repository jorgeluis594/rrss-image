# On-screen text and subtitles

Text is the hook for viewers with the sound off and keeps attention for everyone
else. There are two kinds, and they must not compete:

- **On-screen text** (`texto_pantalla` in `VIDEO.md`): hook, keywords, numbers, CTA.
  Copied exactly from the script.
- **Subtitles**: what is spoken, in short blocks, synchronized with the voice.

## On-screen text

- The hook text is visible from frame 0 and stays until it has been read.
- Prices, offers, conditions, and the CTA are character-for-character as in
  `VIDEO.md`. Proofread every word and number in the rendered draft.
- No more than 5–10 words per second of reading time, per TikTok's guidance; short
  phrases of 3–8 words.
- Large, heavy type with high contrast: light text with an outline, shadow, or a
  solid box over busy footage.

## Subtitles

- Transcribe what was actually said in the selected take. Natural lines may differ
  from the script; subtitles follow the audio. If a `literal: sí` line was changed
  in the take, report it instead of subtitling the script wording.
- One to four words per block, one or two lines, timed to the voice. A block's
  duration is reading time, not the length of the voice; no block overlaps the next
  one or the on-screen text that follows.
- Highlight one keyword per block in a brand color when it has enough contrast.
- Do not subtitle a line that is already written on screen at that moment: it
  duplicates text in the same place.

## Typography and brand

- Use the typography and colors in `MARCA.md`. If none are defined, use one bold,
  highly legible sans serif for both kinds and record the choice in `edicion.md`.
- At most two type styles per video (one for hook and on-screen text, one for
  subtitles), the same across the brand's videos for recognition.
- The logo, if used, goes at the end or small in a safe corner; never as an opening.

## Safe area (1080×1920)

Platform controls cover parts of the frame. Keep text and faces out of:

- about 250 px at the bottom (caption, sound, CTA buttons),
- about 100–250 px at the top (tabs, search),
- about 150 px on the right (like, comment, share buttons).

Place the hook in the central 60% of the height. Subtitles sit in the lower-middle
area, above the bottom margin, and move up when they would cover the product or a face.
These values are approximate and change with the apps: verify on an extracted frame
with the safe-area overlay described in `exportacion.md`.
