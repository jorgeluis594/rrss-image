# Effect library

Descriptions of effects, in words. There is no code here: the editor writes each
brand's effect scripts from these descriptions when a video needs them. Choose with
the user which ones the brand uses, then copy them into `ESTILO-VIDEO.md` in Spanish,
adapted to the brand (its colors, its words, what the user asked for), keeping every
field. A description must be precise enough that two scripts written from it are
recognizably the same effect, and open enough («May vary») that they are not identical.

Every effect serves the same end: win the viewer's attention or keep it. «Function»
says how. An effect is available to a brand only when its «Dial and level» is at or
below the brand's levels; a ficha that names two dials needs both.

Sounds are not effects with scripts: a pop on a tap or a figure and a whoosh on a
transition are audio cues the editor places, within the adornos dial.

## Hook

### Titular de apertura
- **Function:** stop the swipe. Tells the viewer in the first frame what the video is about.
- **Dial and level:** texto 1 or more.
- **What the viewer sees:** a short headline in the upper-middle of the frame, already
  there in the first frame, on a solid dark or light box. One part of the phrase is in
  the accent color. It settles in the first third of a second and leaves when the
  speaker has said it.
- **Measures:** up to 8 words on one to three lines; visible 2.5–3.5 s; fades out in 0.2 s.
- **May vary:** box or outlined text without box; which part carries the accent; how it
  settles (a slight shrink into place, a short rise, or simply there).
- **Never changes:** readable in frame 0, exact wording from the script, clear of the face.
- **Receives:** the lines, the accented part, brand colors and typography, vertical position.
- **Check:** frame 0 and one frame at 1 s: full text, legible, inside the safe area.

### Acercamiento de apertura
- **Function:** stop the swipe. A sudden move toward the speaker says something is starting.
- **Dial and level:** movimiento 2 or more.
- **What the viewer sees:** within the first second the picture jumps closer to the
  speaker's face and stays there until the first cut.
- **Measures:** starts between 0.3 and 0.6 s; reaches 125–140 % in about 0.2 s.
- **May vary:** how close, how fast, whether it eases back slightly afterwards.
- **Never changes:** anchored on the face, never crops the eyes or the hook text.
- **Receives:** the closeness, the anchor point, when it starts and ends.
- **Check:** one frame before and one after: face whole, hook text untouched.

### Pregunta que se escribe
- **Function:** stop the swipe. Text appearing letter by letter makes the eye wait for the end.
- **Dial and level:** texto 2 or more.
- **What the viewer sees:** the hook phrase types itself out in the upper-middle of the
  frame, with or without a cursor, and stays complete until the speaker has said it.
- **Measures:** fully written within 1.2 s; the first two words are present in frame 0.
- **May vary:** cursor or none; by letter or by word; box or outline.
- **Never changes:** the first words are readable in frame 0; exact wording from the script.
- **Receives:** the phrase, brand colors and typography, vertical position.
- **Check:** frame 0 (first words present) and one frame after it completes.

## Following the voice

### Subtítulos tranquilos
- **Function:** follow the voice. Serves viewers without sound without pulling the eye
  away from the picture.
- **Dial and level:** texto 1.
- **What the viewer sees:** phrases of a few words in the lower-middle of the frame, in
  sentence case with a dark outline, changing with the voice. They appear and
  disappear without movement or with a very short fade.
- **Measures:** up to 5 words per phrase; one or two lines; medium size.
- **May vary:** fade or cut between phrases; the spoken word in the accent color or none.
- **Never changes:** legible throughout, centered on the frame, never over the face, in
  sync with the voice.
- **Receives:** the words with their times, brand colors and typography, vertical position.
- **Check:** two frames from different phrases: complete text inside the safe area.

### Subtítulos que saltan
- **Function:** follow the voice. Keeps the eye busy and serves viewers without sound.
- **Dial and level:** texto 2, movimiento 2 or more.
- **What the viewer sees:** a few words at a time in the center, in heavy capitals with
  a dark outline. Each group drops in with a small bounce and stays still. The word
  being spoken turns to the accent color and grows slightly; when it ends it returns
  to its size.
- **Measures:** groups of up to 3 words; the entry lasts under half a second; the
  spoken word grows 10–15 %.
- **May vary:** the tilt of each group, the kind of bounce, whether the spoken word
  has a box or only color.
- **Never changes:** legible throughout, centered on the frame, never over the face, in
  sync with the voice.
- **Receives:** the words with their times, brand colors and typography, vertical position.
- **Check:** one frame mid-entry and one with the group still: complete text inside
  the safe area in both.

### Palabra por palabra
- **Function:** follow the voice. Each word replaces the last, so the viewer keeps
  reading at the speaker's pace.
- **Dial and level:** texto 3.
- **What the viewer sees:** one or two words at a time, very large, in the center. Each
  one appears exactly when it is spoken and replaces the previous one.
- **Measures:** 1–2 words; each appears in under 0.15 s; size up to twice that of
  phrase captions.
- **May vary:** a pop or a hard cut on each word; accent color on keywords only or on
  every other word.
- **Never changes:** in sync with the voice to the word, never over the face, never
  wider than the safe area.
- **Receives:** the words with their times, the keywords, brand colors and typography.
- **Check:** three consecutive words, one frame each: one word per frame, whole.

### Subtítulos con caja en la palabra
- **Function:** follow the voice. A box that travels along the phrase shows where the
  speaker is.
- **Dial and level:** texto 2.
- **What the viewer sees:** the phrase stays on screen and a rounded box in the accent
  color sits behind the word being spoken, moving to the next word as the voice advances.
- **Measures:** phrases of up to 4 words; the box moves in under 0.12 s.
- **May vary:** the box slides or jumps; capitals or sentence case.
- **Never changes:** the text over the box keeps enough contrast to read; in sync with
  the voice.
- **Receives:** the words with their times, brand colors and typography, vertical position.
- **Check:** two frames in the same phrase with the box on different words.

## Emphasis

### Empuje lento
- **Function:** keep attention. A shot that moves slightly never looks frozen.
- **Dial and level:** movimiento 1 or more.
- **What the viewer sees:** inside each shot the picture comes closer very slowly from
  the first frame to the last; the viewer feels it more than sees it.
- **Measures:** 3–5 % over the whole shot, at constant speed.
- **May vary:** toward or away from the subject; the anchor point.
- **Never changes:** no visible start or stop; restarts on every cut.
- **Receives:** the shots with their start and end, the amount, the anchor point.
- **Check:** first and last frame of one shot: slightly different framing, nothing cropped.

### Acercamiento suave
- **Function:** emphasis. Underlines one moment without startling.
- **Dial and level:** movimiento 2, ritmo 1 or more.
- **What the viewer sees:** on a key phrase the picture glides closer to the speaker
  and stays until the next cut.
- **Measures:** reaches 110–115 % in 0.4–0.6 s.
- **May vary:** how close; whether it returns before the cut.
- **Never changes:** anchored on the face or the product; starts as the key word begins.
- **Receives:** when it starts and ends, the closeness, the anchor point.
- **Check:** one frame before and one at the end of the move.

### Acercamiento de golpe
- **Function:** emphasis. A sudden jump closer marks the word that matters.
- **Dial and level:** movimiento 3, ritmo 2 or more.
- **What the viewer sees:** exactly on a keyword (the brand, the price, the call to
  action) the picture jumps closer to the face and holds until the next cut.
- **Measures:** reaches 115–120 % in 0.15–0.2 s.
- **May vary:** how close; a tiny settle after the jump.
- **Never changes:** anchored on the face; lands on the word, not after it; never two
  within 1.5 s.
- **Receives:** when it starts and ends, the closeness, the anchor point.
- **Check:** one frame before and one after: face whole and centered.

### Abrir desde un detalle
- **Function:** keep attention. Starting close on an object and opening out reveals
  the scene and resets the eye.
- **Dial and level:** movimiento 2 or more.
- **What the viewer sees:** a shot starts tight on a detail (a clock, a product, a
  hand) and opens out to the full picture.
- **Measures:** from 130–140 % to 100 % in 0.6–0.8 s.
- **May vary:** the speed; whether it opens fully or stops a little short.
- **Never changes:** the detail is recognizable in the first frame; ends still.
- **Receives:** when it starts and ends, the starting closeness, the anchor point.
- **Check:** first frame (the detail) and one frame after it settles.

### Palabra clave en pantalla
- **Function:** emphasis. One word on screen fixes what the viewer should remember.
- **Dial and level:** texto 2 or more, ritmo 2 or more.
- **What the viewer sees:** when a key word or figure is spoken, it appears alone and
  large in the middle of the frame for a moment, then leaves.
- **Measures:** 1–3 words; visible 0.8–1.5 s; enters in under 0.3 s.
- **May vary:** the entry (pop, rise, stamp); box, outline, or highlighter.
- **Never changes:** wording from the script or from what is said; captions hide while
  it is up; never over the face.
- **Receives:** the text, when it starts and ends, brand colors and typography, position.
- **Check:** one frame mid-entry and one at rest.

## Showing the product or the app

### Pantalla de la app en escena
- **Function:** make a demonstration readable. A raw screen recording is flat; framed
  and centered, the viewer knows where to look.
- **Dial and level:** movimiento 1 or more.
- **What the viewer sees:** the screen recording centered on a brand-colored
  background, large enough to read, with room above for a headline. It enters once
  and then plays.
- **Measures:** the recording takes 55–65 % of the frame height; enters in 0.4–0.75 s.
- **May vary:** with a device frame or as a rounded card without one, as the brand's
  rules say; entry from below, a fade, or a slight rotation that settles; a faint float
  while it stays.
- **Never changes:** centered on the frame; the interface is not cropped or covered;
  the recording plays at real speed unless the plan says otherwise.
- **Receives:** the recording and where it starts, the background colors, when it
  starts and ends.
- **Check:** one frame mid-entry and one at rest: recording whole, centered, legible.

### Titular con resaltador
- **Function:** tie what is said to what is shown. The viewer reads the phrase while
  watching the demonstration.
- **Dial and level:** texto 2 or more.
- **What the viewer sees:** above the product or the app, the spoken phrase appears
  line by line as each line is said. Behind the key word, a marker stroke in the
  accent color unrolls from left to right at the moment that word is spoken.
- **Measures:** up to 3 lines of up to 5 words; each line enters in about 0.3 s; the
  marker unrolls in about 0.3 s.
- **May vary:** the lines rise or fade in; the marker straight or slightly tilted;
  marker or underline.
- **Never changes:** the words are what the speaker says; one highlighted word or pair
  per line; captions hide while it is up.
- **Receives:** the lines with their times, the highlighted word and its time, brand
  colors and typography.
- **Check:** one frame with the marker half unrolled and one with all lines in.

### Onda en el toque
- **Function:** show where to look. A tap on a recording is invisible without it.
- **Dial and level:** movimiento 1 or more.
- **What the viewer sees:** at each tap on the app, a ring in the accent color grows
  from the touched point and fades.
- **Measures:** lasts about 0.5 s; grows to about a sixth of the screen width.
- **May vary:** one ring or two; filled or outline.
- **Never changes:** centered on the real tap position; starts just before the
  interface reacts.
- **Receives:** each tap's time and position on the recording.
- **Check:** one frame mid-wave: centered on the control that was tapped.

### Acercamiento al control
- **Function:** show where to look. Brings the viewer to the button that matters.
- **Dial and level:** movimiento 2 or more.
- **What the viewer sees:** just before an important tap, the screen recording grows
  toward the control being used; after the result appears, it returns to its size.
- **Measures:** 15–20 % closer; in and out in about 0.4 s each.
- **May vary:** how close; whether the headline above fades while it is close.
- **Never changes:** the control and its result stay fully visible; returns before
  the next headline.
- **Receives:** when it goes in and out, the point it approaches.
- **Check:** one frame at the closest point: control and result both visible.

## Figure or price

### Cartel de cifra
- **Function:** make the number land. A price said in passing is forgotten; alone on
  screen it is remembered.
- **Dial and level:** texto 1 or more.
- **What the viewer sees:** a full-screen card in a brand color. A small line comes
  first, then the figure enters alone and is by far the largest thing on screen,
  then a short line below it. An underline or marker in the accent color unrolls
  under the figure.
- **Measures:** visible 1.5–3 s; the figure takes about a quarter of the frame width
  per character at most; the three parts enter within 1 s.
- **May vary:** the figure's entry (bounce, rise, fade, as the movimiento dial allows);
  underline, marker, or none; the logo small at the bottom or not.
- **Never changes:** figure, currency, and conditions exactly as in the script; nothing
  else competes with the figure.
- **Receives:** the three texts, brand colors and typography, the logo file if used.
- **Check:** one frame as the figure enters and one at rest: every character correct.

## Change of act

### Barrido de color
- **Function:** mark the turn from problem to solution, so the viewer feels a new part
  starts.
- **Dial and level:** adornos 2 or more, movimiento 2 or more.
- **What the viewer sees:** one or two slanted bands in brand colors cross the frame
  and cover the cut; when they pass, the next scene is there.
- **Measures:** about 0.35 s in total; the cut happens while the frame is covered.
- **May vary:** direction; one band or two; the slant.
- **Never changes:** at most once per video; the frame is fully covered at the cut.
- **Receives:** the time of the cut, the band colors.
- **Check:** the frame at the cut (fully covered) and one frame after.

### Fundido
- **Function:** mark a change of act quietly.
- **Dial and level:** adornos 1 or more.
- **What the viewer sees:** the outgoing scene dissolves into the incoming one, or
  through a brand color.
- **Measures:** 0.3–0.5 s.
- **May vary:** direct or through a color.
- **Never changes:** never inside a sentence; at most once per video.
- **Receives:** the time of the cut, the color if used.
- **Check:** the middle frame: both scenes or the color, no black flash.

### Deslizamiento
- **Function:** mark a change of act with direction, as if moving to the next thing.
- **Dial and level:** adornos 2 or more, movimiento 1 or more.
- **What the viewer sees:** the new scene pushes the old one out of the frame sideways
  or upward.
- **Measures:** 0.3–0.4 s.
- **May vary:** the direction; a soft or a bouncing stop, as the movimiento dial allows.
- **Never changes:** at most once per video; both scenes move together with no gap.
- **Receives:** the time of the cut, the direction.
- **Check:** the middle frame: both scenes side by side, no gap between them.

## Closing

### Tarjeta de cierre
- **Function:** turn attention into action. The viewer leaves knowing what to do.
- **Dial and level:** texto 1 or more.
- **What the viewer sees:** while the call to action is spoken, a card with the logo
  and the call to action enters in the lower-middle of the frame, over the speaker,
  and stays until the last frame.
- **Measures:** enters in 0.4–0.7 s; visible at least 1.5 s; up to 6 words.
- **May vary:** the entry (rise, fade, bounce, as the movimiento dial allows); a
  colored border or none; logo left or above.
- **Never changes:** wording exactly as in the script; present in the last frame;
  clear of the bottom controls and of the speaker's face.
- **Receives:** the texts, the logo file, brand colors and typography, position.
- **Check:** one frame mid-entry and the last frame of the video.

## Decoration

### Mascota que aparece
- **Function:** personality. A recognizable character reacting to the story makes the
  brand memorable.
- **Dial and level:** adornos 3; only when the brand's rules allow a character.
- **What the viewer sees:** the brand's character appears at a beat of the story,
  reacts to it (tired at the problem, celebrating at the result, pointing at the call
  to action), and leaves.
- **Measures:** at most three appearances per video; each 1–2 s; takes up to a third
  of the frame width.
- **May vary:** where it comes from (peeks from below, drops from above, pops in
  place); a small idle movement while it stays.
- **Never changes:** only the brand's own artwork; never over the face, the captions,
  or the product.
- **Receives:** the image of each pose, when it starts and ends, position and size.
- **Check:** one frame per appearance at rest: nothing important covered.

### Fondo con formas lentas
- **Function:** keep a still composition alive behind a card or a demonstration.
- **Dial and level:** adornos 3, movimiento 2 or more.
- **What the viewer sees:** behind a card or the app, a soft gradient in brand colors
  with two or three blurred shapes drifting slowly.
- **Measures:** shapes move a few dozen pixels over several seconds.
- **May vary:** number, size, and path of the shapes.
- **Never changes:** low contrast, so text and interface over it stay legible.
- **Receives:** the background colors.
- **Check:** two frames 2 s apart: slightly different, text equally legible.

### Flotación
- **Function:** keep attention. An element that stays on screen for seconds does not
  look frozen.
- **Dial and level:** movimiento 2 or more.
- **What the viewer sees:** a card, a screen recording, or a sticker that stays more
  than 2 s drifts a few pixels up and down, with a barely visible tilt.
- **Measures:** a few pixels; under one degree; one cycle every 3–4 s.
- **May vary:** amount and rhythm.
- **Never changes:** never applied to captions or to text being read.
- **Receives:** which element, the amount.
- **Check:** two frames 1.5 s apart: the element slightly displaced, sharp in both.
