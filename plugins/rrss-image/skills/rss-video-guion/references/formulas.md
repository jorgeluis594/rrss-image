# Narrative formulas for short videos

A short video is a compressed story: someone wants something, an obstacle appears,
and there is a visible change by the end. Every formula below follows that arc
(hook → tension → turn or proof → result → CTA). Use the slug in `formula:`.

Show, don't tell: every claim needs an image that proves it. If a scene only says
something, add an action, detail shot, or result that shows it.

| Slug | Structure | Typical length | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `problema-solucion` | Recognizable pain → why it gets worse → product resolves it → result | 15–30 s | Direct-response promotion, products that fix a clear problem |
| `antes-despues` | Final result first → how it was before → fast process → result again | 15–45 s | Visible transformations: food, beauty, cleaning, repairs, spaces |
| `detras-de-camaras` | "Así se hace…" → steps with sensory detail → finished product | 20–60 s | Craft, restaurants, workshops, factories, service quality |
| `historia-cliente` | Customer situation → conflict → experience with the brand → change | 30–60 s | Trust, high-value services. Real customers with consent only |
| `lista-tutorial` | Promise of N items → one per block → best one last → CTA | 20–45 s | Authority, education, B2B, finance, advice |
| `pov-sketch` | Everyday situation acted with local humor → twist → product as punchline | 10–30 s | Reach, community, brand personality |

## Selection when delegated

Classify the main message in this order and record the criterion:

1. A visible transformation exists and can be filmed or generated faithfully → `antes-despues`.
2. A real customer with consent will tell their experience → `historia-cliente`.
3. The message is how something is made or the care behind it → `detras-de-camaras`.
4. The message is advice or several useful points → `lista-tutorial`.
5. The message is a pain the product resolves → `problema-solucion`.
6. The goal is reach or personality and the brand tone allows humor → `pov-sketch`.

The presence of a product or a price does not by itself decide the formula.

## Rules for every formula

- Start in the middle of the action; context comes later, in one sentence.
- One message per video. A second idea is a second video.
- Do not answer the hook's question before the result block. Open small loops
  ("y lo tercero es lo que nadie hace") to carry attention through the middle.
- Speak to one person ("tú"), not to "nuestros clientes".
- Prefer a real, recurring person of the business (owner, staff) as protagonist so
  the audience recognizes the brand by the person.
- The product is the tool of the turn. It opens the video only when the product
  itself is the strongest visual hook.
- One concrete CTA: «Escríbenos X por DM», «link en el perfil», «guárdalo». The CTA
  must match a channel the brand actually uses.
- When possible, the last shot or line connects with the first, so the video loops.

## Formula details

### problema-solucion
Hook names or shows the pain. Tension: a short, relatable consequence, not a lecture.
Turn: the product in use, shown, not described. Result: the concrete benefit.
Avoid exaggerated suffering or results the brief does not support.

### antes-despues
Open with the "after" or a split-second preview of it. Then the "before", then a
compressed process (timelapse, speed ramp, match cuts). Before and after must be the
same subject and both real or faithfully produced; never fake a transformation.

### detras-de-camaras
Lead with the most striking moment of the process (fire, pour, cut, reveal). Use
detail and POV shots and real sound. End with the finished product and how to get it.

### historia-cliente
Requires a real customer and consent; otherwise use `pov-sketch` as a dramatization.
The customer's words are not rewritten into claims they did not make.

### lista-tutorial
State the number in the hook. Each item is its own scene with a visual change. Put
the strongest item last and announce that it is coming.

### pov-sketch
Text like «POV: …» or a recognizable situation. Characters are clearly acted
(`representacion: dramatizacion`). Humor must respect the restrictions in `MARCA.md`;
avoid jokes about groups of people.
