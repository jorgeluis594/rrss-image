# Script review

Run this review before presenting a script for approval and again after every
change. Fix blocking defects before showing the script. Report communication and
naturalness issues that remain as decisions for the user, not as hidden compromises.

## 1. Blocking defects

| Defect | Check and remedy |
| --- | --- |
| Invented commercial fact | Every price, offer, date, result, attribute, or number in a line or on screen has a source in the brief. Remove or ask. |
| Actor or generated person presented as a real customer, employee, or testimonial | `representacion` is `dramatizacion` and nothing in the lines says otherwise, or the person is real with consent. |
| Product missing, wrong, or invented | The product appears where the story needs it, with a real reference for production; no parts or results the buyer would not get. |
| Hook not paid off | The video delivers what the hook promises, in the `resultado` scene. |
| CTA on a channel the brand does not use | The CTA matches `MARCA.md` or the brief. |
| Timing does not fit | Lines fit their scenes at ~2.5 words/s and the total is within ±10% of the target. |
| Scene cannot be produced | Every scene has a location, talent, props, and a source that the brief says are available. |
| Restriction in `MARCA.md` violated | Prohibited claims, words, humor, or assets are absent. |

## 2. Communication

- One main message; a viewer can repeat it in one sentence after watching.
- The first frame has movement or a face and the hook text; no logo, greeting, or intro.
- The hook's premise is resolved by second 3; the payoff is not given away early.
- Every claim has a visual proof in its scene (show, don't tell).
- Something changes visibly every 2–4 s; no shot is planned longer than 4 s.
- On-screen text is short and placed in the safe area; it does not duplicate every word
  of the spoken line.
- The CTA is a single concrete action.

## 3. Naturalness and specificity

- Read every line aloud at the expected pace. Lines that sound read, formal, or
  corporate («soluciones integrales», «nuestros clientes») are rewritten in the
  brand's register.
- The script could not be posted unchanged by a competitor: it shows this business's
  people, place, product details, or way of working.
- Humor and local references fit the audience in the brief and the tone in `MARCA.md`.
- Repetition across a series is fine when it builds recognition (same presenter,
  same opening style); it is not fine when it hides product differences.
