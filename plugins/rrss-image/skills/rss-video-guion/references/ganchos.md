# Hooks

The hook is the first 0–3 seconds. Viewers decide to stop scrolling in roughly the
first 1.5 s and decide whether the video interests them by second 3. TikTok's own
guidance asks for the value proposition in the first 3 seconds and the hook within 6.

A hook has three layers that work together. The first frame must carry them because
many viewers start with the sound off:

1. **First frame**: movement, a face, or an unexpected image. Never a logo, a black
   screen, an intro animation, or a greeting.
2. **On-screen text**: 3–8 words, large, readable without sound.
3. **Spoken line**: states or completes the promise in one sentence.

## Hook types

| Type | Pattern | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `resultado-primero` | Show the "after" and promise how | Plato terminado humeando: «Esto se hace en 10 minutos.» |
| `pregunta-error` | Name a common mistake or ask about it | «¿Sigues guardando el pan en la refri? Lo estás secando.» |
| `afirmacion` | A bold, true, verifiable statement | «La mitad de lo que pagas por un sofá es la tela.» (only with a source) |
| `curiosidad` | Open a gap the video will close | «Nadie te cuenta esto cuando abres un negocio.» |
| `accion-en-curso` | Start in the middle of a scene, no introduction | Manos cortando, algo que cae, una puerta que se abre |
| `pov` | Put the viewer in a situation | «POV: es 28 y ya no te queda sueldo.» |
| `numero` | Announce a list | «3 cosas que revisar antes de comprar zapatillas.» |

The three variants for one video use three different types.

## Rules

- Resolve the premise by second 3. The payoff comes at the end.
- The hook must be true to the video. Never promise a result the brief does not
  support or the video does not deliver.
- Cut the warm-up: «Hola, soy…», «En este video…», «Hoy les traigo…» are removed.
  The brand is recognized by the person and the style, not by an introduction.
- Prefer conflict over context, and a face to camera or a striking product moment
  over a generic B-roll opening.
- A claim with a number needs a source recorded in the brief; otherwise rephrase it
  without the number.
- Use the audience's own words and slang as recorded in the brief or `MARCA.md`.

## Describing a variant

For each variant write `primer_frame` precisely enough to film or generate it: who or
what is in frame, shot size, the action already in progress, and where the text sits
(inside the central safe area; see `guion-tecnico.md`). Then `voz` and `texto_pantalla`.
